Help

Click the floor to walk. Click the anvil to smith, the range to cook. Right-click the chest to upgrade it. Scroll to zoom out for the roof and garden.

The camera follows you. You can walk behind the counter. Scroll or −/= to zoom; arrow keys change the angle. Zoom far out to see the roof and garden.

Click the anvil to smith Melee, Magic, Ranged, or Potions. Click the cooking range for Food. Finished pieces land in the chest. Right-click the chest to upgrade its space.

Travelers line up at the counter. Click the one at the front to sell, offer a different chest item at a reduced price, refuse, or buy a scrap they offer.

Click a table, shelf, or armour stand, then place a ware from the chest. Food sits on the wall shelves. Click furniture for Move or Rotate — wares travel with the piece.

The expand button beside Save buys extra rooms. Save and Load write a JSON file on your computer.
