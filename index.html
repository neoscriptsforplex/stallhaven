    Keep the store

    You keep the Store of Geilenor. Click the floor to walk — the camera follows you. Scroll or −/= to zoom; arrow keys change the angle.

    Click the anvil to craft a sword, bow, staff, or armour. Finished gear goes in the wooden chest.

    Travelers come through the front door and line up at the counter. Click the traveler at the front to sell, refuse, or buy a scrap they offer — then they leave and the line advances.

    Click the wooden chest to see your inventory. Click a table, wall shelf, or armour stand — it lights gold — then place a ware. Armour stands wear helm, body, and legs together.

    Spend gold to restock materials. Drag a model onto the drop zone if you want a custom table or ware look — nothing uploaded leaves your browser.
