Help

Click the floor to walk. Click the anvil to smith, the range to cook, the cauldron to brew. Right-click furniture to move or rotate it. Scroll to zoom out for the roof and garden.

The camera follows you. You can walk behind the counter. Scroll or −/= to zoom; arrow keys change the angle. Zoom far out to see the roof and garden.

Click the anvil to smith Melee, Magic, or Ranged (Weapons and Armour). Click the cooking range for Food. Click a placed cauldron for Potions. Finished pieces land in the chest. Right-click the chest to upgrade its space, move, or rotate it.

Travelers line up at the counter. Click the one at the front to sell, offer a different chest item at a reduced price, craft what they asked for, refuse, or buy a scrap they offer.

Click a table or wall shelf to select it. Right-click a table or shelf and choose Display item… to place a chest ware on it (four slots per shelf). Food and potions sit on wall shelves. Right-click furniture for Move or Rotate — wares travel with the piece. The counter only moves from a right-click.

Build unlocks extra rooms, tables, mannequins, and a cauldron. Extra tables and mannequins start at 500g, then ×3 for each further purchase of that type. The fullscreen button (top left of the toolbar) fills the window. Save and Load write a JSON file on your computer. The music-note button (right of Load) plays a looping track you upload.
