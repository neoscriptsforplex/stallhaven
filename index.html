Crafting

Drop a .glb / .gltf or click to choose. Tag it furniture or ware.

    Click the floor to walk — including behind the counter. Click the anvil to craft. Click the traveler at the front of the line to trade.

    Keep Rune Craft

    You keep Rune Craft. Click the floor to walk — the camera follows you. You can walk behind the counter. Scroll or −/= to zoom; arrow keys change the angle.

    Click the anvil to craft under Melee, Magic, Ranged, Food, or Potions. Bronze gear, a Staff, Blue d'hide, and Bread start unlocked. Craft enough of a piece to open the next tier in that line.

    Travelers come through the front door and line up on the customer side of the counter. Click the traveler at the front to sell, refuse, or buy a scrap they offer — then they leave and the line advances.

    Click the wooden chest to see your inventory. Click a table, wall shelf, or armour stand — it lights gold — then place a ware. Food sits on the wall shelves.

    Save and Load in the top right write a JSON file on your computer. Spend gold to restock materials. Drag a model onto the drop zone if you want a custom table or ware look — nothing uploaded leaves your browser.
