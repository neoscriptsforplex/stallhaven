    Help

    Controls: Use Left click (tap on phone) to move, right click (Double tap on phone) to open menus on items and upgrade them. Use middle mouse button or arrow keys to control the camera. On a phone: Tap to walk. Swipe with one finger to turn the camera, pinch to zoom. Long-press (half a second) or double-tap an item to open its menu. In crafting, tap a recipe to preview it, then press 1x, 5x or Max to craft. The Goal: Use the Anvil to craft items or your townsfolk. You will need to cut down trees outside for wood, pick flax on the grass, and mine ores in the dungeon outside. Build crafting stations to help you make more items. In the build menu you can buy the below crafting stations: Furnace (smelt ores to bars) Cooking Range (make food) Fletching Bench (craft Bows, Arrows and Ammo) Spinning Wheel (turn flax into cloth or bowstring) Loom (craft armour) Cauldron (craft potions) Upgrade your shop after earning money. Buy more shelves, mannequins, tables and expand your shop! Save your game file before closing game. This will allow you to load it back again.

    Play from your Home Screen: iPhone or iPad: open the game in Safari (it has to be Safari), tap Share (the square with an up arrow), scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. It then opens full-screen from its own icon. Android: in Chrome tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).

    Build

    Furnace

    Smelt ores into metal bars. Place it on the floor snap grid, then confirm. Free.

    Cooking Range

    Bake food. Place it on the floor snap grid, then confirm. Free.

    Potter Wheel

    Turn soft clay into clay. Place it on the floor snap grid, then confirm.

    Costs 10,000 gp. Steel Nails ×12 · Willow Plank ×3.

    Sawmill

    Saw logs into planks. Place it on the floor snap grid, then confirm. Click it afterward to saw.

    Costs 20,000 gp.

    Loom

    Weave flax into cloth, then ranged and magic armour. Place it on the floor snap grid, then confirm.

    Costs 30,000 gp. Mithril Nails ×12 · Maple Plank ×3.

    Spinning Wheel

    Spin flax into bow string. Place it on the floor snap grid, then confirm. The wheel turns while a string is spinning.

    Costs 70,000 gp. Mithril Nails ×15 · Maple Plank ×4.

    Fletching Bench

    Craft ranged weapons and ammo. Place it on the floor snap grid, then confirm.

    Costs 100,000 gp. Adamant Nails ×20 · Yew Plank ×4.

    Portal

    Opens a grass rune field. Place it on the grass or the floor snap grid, then confirm. Click it afterward to enter.

    Costs 500,000 gp. Rune Nails ×25 · Magic Plank ×6.

    Cauldron

    Unlocks potion crafting. Place it on the floor snap grid, then confirm. Click it afterward to brew.

    Costs 5,000,000 gp. Dragon Nails ×30 · Magic Plank ×8 · Steel Bar ×1,000.

    Farming Patch

    An outdoor 2×2 plot. You can own six. Place it on open grass, clear of the path and flower beds, then confirm. Click a placed patch to grow herbs. Right-click it to move or delete it.

    You can own six farming patches. Cost: 1,000,000 gp Needs: Iron Nails ×6 (have 0) · Oak Plank ×2 (have 0)

    Table

    Buy another display table. There is no maximum. The first extra costs 500g; each further table costs three times the last. Starting tables do not count. Delete up to two tables to replace them for free.

    No maximum. First extra table costs 500g. Bronze Nails ×8 · Plank ×4.

    Shelf

    Buy another wall shelf. There is no maximum. Place it on open wall. The starting three shelves are included; extras cost 500g, then ×3. Delete a shelf from its right-click menu — a deleted included shelf can be replaced for free until three are placed.

    No maximum. First 3 shelves are included. The next costs 500g. Iron Nails ×10 · Oak Plank ×3.

    Mannequin

    Buy another armour mannequin. There is no maximum. The first extra costs 500g; each further mannequin costs three times the last. Starting mannequins do not count. Delete up to two mannequins to replace them for free.

    No maximum. First extra mannequin costs 500g. Steel Nails ×12 · Willow Plank ×2.
